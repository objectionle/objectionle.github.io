Objectionle

Objectionle is a free daily courtroom puzzle. Read up to six clues about a moment at trial and name the objection, hearsay exception, or response. It covers hearsay, leading questions, relevance, authentication, character evidence, privileges and more, based on the Federal Rules of Evidence.

Objectionle is an educational game, not legal advice. The applicable rules depend on the jurisdiction, court, and proceeding.
